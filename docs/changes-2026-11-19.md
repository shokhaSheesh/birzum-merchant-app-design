## Admin front (`birzum_react_admin`)
- **A15.** Remove «Eslatma yuborish» and «Kartadan yechish» everywhere in Birzum (debt page, Qarzdorlar table, client page) and the «Kartadan pul yechish» permission. Rename the menu item to «Toʻlovlar». Add the «Plan/fakt» page (`/plan-fact`): a row per nasiya, Plan / Fakt / Farq per month, total, today's debt; all rows on one page with a sticky header and first column.
- **A16.** In Fortuna and Vodiy:
  - Replace «Kechikkanlar» with «Qarzdorlar», the same board and loan page as Birzum's. The call form adds «Kim bilan gaplashildi» (the borrower or a contact).
  - Add «Plan/fakt» per loan.
  - Add a «Hisobot» tab on Toʻlovlar: promises by month and operators, the same as Birzum's.
  - Remove the reminder and «Qayta yechish».
  - Add the role «Undiruvchi (mikroqarz)».

## Backend
| For | Need |
|---|---|
| A15, A16 | Plan / fakt per nasiya (Birzum) and per loan (each brand), by month: plan = what was unpaid on the 1st of the month for that month's instalments, fakt = money in that month; plus the export. Or the schedule as of a date plus payments, so the front can compute it |
| A16 | Debt cases per loan for each brand (same model as Birzum's A14): list, one case, status change with reason, call record with **who was called** (borrower or contact), next call, operator, history |
| A16 | Hisobot per brand: promises by month (given, due, kept) and per operator (calls, reached, promises, kept, money in on assigned loans), for a period. Birzum's Hisobot (A14) needs the same; nothing in the swagger yet |
| A16 | Drop the reminder and re-charge endpoints from the admin: `POST /admin/apps/{app}/loans/{id}/reminders`, `POST /admin/apps/{app}/loan-payments/{id}/retry` (both mocks today) |
