## Decisions

### Signing a contract (00:00–36:00)
- **Signing method: SMS OTP.** Bobur first suggested MyID. FlexSoft checks the OTP, and MyID costs about 200 times more, so OTP was chosen. Birzum still has to confirm this with FlexSoft.
- **The OTP is sent when the client presses «Tasdiqlash» in the app,** not when the shop confirms. That way it doesn't expire while the client is away.
- **The same push → sheet → OTP flow** is used for a client-made order (after the shop confirms within 24 h), a shop-made application, and a cancel or refund.
- **Not confirmed → cancelled at 00:00 when the day changes.**
- **A client without the app** gets an SMS with the App Store / Google Play links.
- **Proof for disputes:** each application in the admin gets the contract/offer attached and history entries with times: offer accepted, «O'zim bajaryapman», OTP. Cyber police sometimes ask for this.

### After the contract is created (36:00–41:00, 1:34–1:38)
- **The 4 cases** (the user's notes):
  1. After the client says «Qabul qildim», the shop's «Mahsulot topshirdim» button works. When the shop presses it, the contract becomes Tasdiqlandi and FlexSoft makes the provodka.
  2. The client may test the item for 3 days, and the shop doesn't press the button yet. If the status hasn't changed after 3 days, it becomes Tasdiqlandi on its own.
  3. If the item comes back within 3 days, the shop sets Bekor qilindi, and the client confirms with OTP.
  4. If the client never says «Qabul qildim» within 3 days, it becomes Tasdiqlandi on its own.
- **One 3-day timer, counted from «Shartnoma yaratildi».** FlexSoft waits 3 days for the shop's confirmation.
- **The handover photo stays.** It shows in the admin on each application and goes to FlexSoft, because limits are often cashed out.

### Refund (41:00–45:00)
- **The refund window is a number of days set in the admin,** counted from Tasdiqlandi. After it, the shop can't pick «Bekor qilindi», and refunds go through the call centre by hand.
- **If the client doesn't confirm a refund,** only the request expires and the contract stays Tasdiqlandi.

### Admin (45:00–1:03)
- **Forgot password:** email → OTP → new password. Needs SMTP; this is the only use of email.
- **Merchants** are read-only from FlexSoft, and branches stay. **Ustama** is read-only.
- **Blocking a client** applies only in the Birzum app, not the FlexSoft blacklist. Sign-in then shows «Bu mijoz bloklangan» and no OTP is sent.
- **Support chats** need a separate person. A role with only Yordam chat and Tayyor javoblar covers it.
- **Remove «Qo'lda to'lov»**: there's no need for cash or bank transfer, only online payments.
- **Analytics:**
  - numbers of clients and merchants, downloads per day and by time, active users
  - the most-viewed products and merchants
  - the sign-up funnel: what % finish and at which step the others stop
- **Viya:** not reviewed yet.

### Client app (1:05–1:44)
- **The SMS code** has letters and digits, a resend every 59 s, and a 15 min block after 3 wrong codes.
- **A new device** triggers an SMS to the old device and has a sessions list. After MyID on a new device, cards are hidden and must be added again.
- **The address:**
  - The client types where they live now («Yashash manzili»), not the propiska. It's stored only in Birzum, for collectors.
  - The MyID propiska arrives in different formats and in two languages, so it's saved but not compared.
  - The fields are viloyat → shahar → tuman (at least one of shahar or tuman) → MFY → street and house.
- **The phone number must belong to the PINFL,** otherwise an error screen.
- **The card's phone number must match the sign-up number.**
- **Before a limit request:** a card and at least 1 contact. A contact can't be the client's own number or a fake one (0000…, 1111…).
- **«Masofadan mumkin emas»** when the shop has been inactive for 30 days.
- **Auto-debit** runs through Atmos and FlexSoft, with an SMS if a debit fails.
- **Phone change:** OTP to the old number, then to the new number, as the nizom says.
